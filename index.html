FAQ

Before you book.

Is this a private service?

Yes. The vehicle is reserved for your booking and is not shared with unrelated passengers.

Do I need to pay online?

No online payment is required to send a booking request. Payment terms are confirmed with you before the ride.

Can I book a custom itinerary?

Yes. Send the cities or stops you want to visit and Albania Ride will confirm availability and a quote.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Send your flight number when booking. Pickup timing can then be coordinated directly with your driver.

How many passengers can travel?

The standard vehicle setup is suitable for up to 4 passengers, subject to luggage size.
