Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Albania Ride collects only the information you choose to provide when requesting a transfer, such as your name, phone number, pickup and destination details, date, time, flight information and notes.

How information is used

Your information is used to respond to your booking request, arrange transportation, coordinate pickup details and provide customer support.

WhatsApp

When you use the booking form, the website opens WhatsApp with your trip details prepared in a message. The message is only sent after you choose to send it in WhatsApp.

Data sharing

Albania Ride does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to provide the requested transport service or when required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@albaniaride.com.
